      Who designs this

      Led by Arya and her mother

      Tiny Masterpieces began with a complaint: the thing you actually want almost never exists. Not the bracelet with your friend’s name on it. Not the card that says the right thing instead of the nearest thing. So Arya and her mother started designing their own.

      Arya brings the ideas — the sketches, the colours, the decision that a set should feel a certain way before anyone works out how to build it. Her mother makes it real: what sizes fit, what holds up in a school bag, what still looks right after a week of being worn every day.

      The Diwali cards are the clearest example. Go looking for a good one and you mostly come back empty-handed — so they drew their own, diyas coloured in by hand. That instinct is the whole business. If the version you want does not exist, design it.

      Which is why nothing here is picked off a shelf. You choose the words, the name and the colours, and it gets made after you ask for it. Nobody else ends up with the same one.

      Questions

      How long does a custom order take?

      A few days for small orders. Party work needs about two weeks so everything gets made properly.

      Why don't party packs have prices yet?

      We're still settling what goes in each one. Send the party details and we'll quote it — and you'll be first to see the pricing when it's set.

      Can you match my party colours?

      Yes. Send a photo of the invite or the colours and we'll bead to match.

      Is there a minimum order?

      No minimum. Bulk pricing starts at 10 of anything.

      How do I pay?

      Venmo or Zelle online, or cash on pickup. Card payment on the site is coming soon. Nothing is due until the order is confirmed.

      Designed by Arya and her mother in Woodbridge, Connecticut. Every piece is made to order in the colours and words you choose, so no two come out the same.
